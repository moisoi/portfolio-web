01 — About

The human behind
the commits.

I'm Messay — a developer who fell in love with the web the first time I viewed page source and realized everything was built by people. Since then I've been obsessed with building things myself: from scrappy prototypes to production systems serving real users.

I work across the stack — designing clean APIs in Node and Python, modeling solid databases, and polishing pixel-perfect interfaces in React. I care deeply about performance budgets, accessibility, and the tiny details users feel but never notice.

When I'm not shipping, you'll find me contributing to open source, mentoring junior devs in Addis Ababa's tech community, or hunting down the city's best buna ☕.
